Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, no difficulty settings, no New Game+, no mission replay — and free roam carries on after the story. Guides put the total at roughly 200–300 hours, most of it on the 100% checklist. Work the three stages in order.

Missable trophies & warnings

Good news first: almost nothing in GTA VI is missable. Free roam continues after the credits, character quests stay available, and the 100% checklist counts everything you did before the story ended. There is one exception to watch.

Possibly missable — the only one

Positive Mental Attitude Bronze

Meditate at every spot across Leonida.

In Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony and accept her meditation. That moment is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy needs it — so do not skip it. Meditate at all 18 spots; meditating with Jason and Lucia together counts twice, and the last spot needs both characters (possible after Coffee Break in Chapter 2). Track it under Progress > Collections > Exploration > Meditations.

Sources: GTABase, Push Square, EGamers (all flag this as the only possibly missable trophy).

Cheats disable trophies

Using a cheat disables trophies and saving for the session. Keep cheats off for the whole trophy run.

Source: GTABase

The Irredeemable profile is permanent

Your Criminal Profile shifts with your actions. Pushing it to Irredeemable cannot be undone, so steer clear while working towards Actions Speak Louder (500 profile actions).

Source: GTABase

Side content can expire

Some optional missions and character quests are only offered for a while. Take on side content as it shows up during the story rather than leaving it all for afterwards.

Source: GTABase

Unconfirmed trophies

Five one-off trophies had no confirmed unlock method when the guides were written: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Each guide says what its description points to and marks the steps unconfirmed.

Source: GTABase

How to use this guide

  • Tick a trophy's box when you earn it; progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) and survives closing the page.
  • Export progress downloads a .json file; import it on another browser or after clearing data to restore your ticks.
  • Spoiler-free mode hides the names and descriptions of hidden story trophies until you tick them earned. Turn it off to see the whole list.
  • This app works fully offline: it loads nothing over the network. Source links open only if you follow them.
